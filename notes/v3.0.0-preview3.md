# OpenTracker 3.0.0 preview 3

A test build of OpenTracker 3, for testing only, not for real work. **Back up your SD card first**, or use a spare card.

OpenTracker is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Polyend.

## Two new card synths

Preview 3 is preview 2 plus two new synths on the card: **1-Type** and **V-Type**. The Full image now has seventeen synths: five built in (ACD, FAT, VAP, PERC, WTFM) and twelve from the card's `Modules/` folder (FM4, JU6, D-Type, CS-Type, A-Type, T-Type, 8-Type, Hand-Type, B-Type, Arc-Type, 1-Type, V-Type). Dirt, BBD Chorus and Tape are built in; Chorus, LoFi and Echo come from the card.

## Files

- `PolyendTrackerOTv300.ptf`: the Full image.
- `Modules-3.0.0.zip`: the twelve card synths and the three card effects. Unzip it at the root of the card; the files land in `/Modules`. Leave out a module you never use.
- `Patches-3.0.0.zip`: the patches for all seventeen synths. Unzip it at the root of the card.
- `OpenTracker-3.0.0-Manual.pdf`: the user manual for 3.0, with a chapter each for 1-Type and V-Type. It covers what changed since 2.x.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums of every file. Check them with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.

## Install

1. Copy `PolyendTrackerOTv300.ptf` to `/Firmware` on the SD card. Keep the file name as it is (24 characters).
2. Unzip `Modules-3.0.0.zip` and `Patches-3.0.0.zip` at the root of the card. **Replace all the `.otm` files** in `/Modules` with the ones from this zip, also the ones you had from preview 2: the modules belong to the image they came with, and older ones may be refused ("needs updating").
3. **Config > Firmware update**, select the image and confirm. If the screen stays dark after the update, press the power button once.
4. To go back, install the stock 1.9.2 image or OpenTracker 2.2.8 the same way.

## New in preview 3

- **1-Type** (`onetype.otm`, `Patches/1TYPE`): a two-oscillator voice with sync, ring and FM, a locked sub, detune, five filter types (LP12, LP24, HP12, BP12 and LPDL), a mod envelope and two LFOs that can follow the song tempo.
- **V-Type** (`vtype.otm`, `Patches/VTYPE`): a swarm of up to seven detuned saws and a second oscillator with sync and ring into two filters, with Drive between them in four routings (Single, Serial, Parallel, Split), two LFOs and a four-slot mod matrix.
- **12 patches each**, in `Patches/1TYPE` and `Patches/VTYPE`: leads, basses, plucks, pads and effects. The pack now has 261 patches.
- **Card memory:** 1-Type (26 KB) and V-Type (22 KB) are the largest card synths. Both fit together, with one card effect (Echo, LoFi or Chorus) beside them; a third card synth next to the pair says "Card memory full".
- **Your .sy1 patches on 1-Type:** the `sy1_to_patch` tool in the firmware sources (`tools/sy1_to_patch`) converts `.sy1` patch files on a computer into 1-Type patches (`Patches/1TYPE/<name>.patch`), with a `<name>.txt` next to each that lists what did not carry over (delay, chorus, the arpeggiator and most of unison: use the track's sends, an insert or the Arp FX instead). The manual's 1-Type chapter has the details.

## Fixes since preview 2

- No other changes to the firmware: everything apart from the two new synths works as in preview 2. The FX a-f names on a step without a note, which follow the synth the sequencer plays there, were already in the preview 2 image.

## Not in this build

- Some songs can still overrun the audio block when a Z switches one slot while another slot with inserts starts many notes in the same step.
- 1-Type's LPDL filter plays as LP24 with a little less resonance.
- CS-Type has no built-in chorus yet; use a Chorus insert.
- After-touch from a MIDI keyboard does not reach Touch yet (use macro 3 or FX CC c).
- Games are removed in 3.x.

## Please test

- **1-Type and V-Type:** play the 24 new patches, alone and on all three slots; change the Model to 1-Type or V-Type while stopped and while playing; turn the knobs on every page while a note plays and listen for clicks, silence or noise.
- **V-Type routings:** Single, Serial, Parallel and Split with high Reso and Drive, and the three Drive curves (Soft, Hard, Fold); the mod matrix on the filters.
- **Card memory:** a project with 1-Type and V-Type on two slots, then turn on Echo, LoFi or Chorus (one fits, a second says "Card memory full"); put a third card synth on the last slot.
- **CPU:** chords and fast arps on both new synths with the CPU meter on, and in a busy song next to inserts.
- **.sy1 imports:** convert your own `.sy1` patches and tell us which ones sound far from the original.
- **Any song:** stuck notes, and the CPU meter on heavy synth steps.

Report what you used, what you did, what you expected and what happened (a photo or a short video helps) on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/opentracker-fw/opentracker-fw.github.io/issues).
