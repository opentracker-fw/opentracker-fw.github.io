# OpenTracker 2.2.4

The current stable release of the 2.x line: an open, community firmware for the original
Polyend Tracker. 2.x brings 16 tracks, three synth slots, stereo samples, Mini-format project
files and USB Storage mode to the original hardware.

OpenTracker is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Polyend.

## Files

- `PolyendTrackerOTv224.ptf`: the firmware image.
- `OpenTracker-2.2.4-Patches.zip`: the preset pack (82 patches: ACD 12, FAT 8, VAP 16, WTFM 30, PERC 16, the same as in 2.2.3). Copy `Patches/` to the root of the card and merge it with what is there.
- `OpenTracker-2.2.4-Themes.zip`: 12 example colour themes. Unzip it to the root of the card; the files land in `/Themes`.
- `OpenTracker-2.2.4-Projects.zip`: the test songs. Copy them to `/Projects` on the card.
- `OpenTracker-2.2.4-Manual.pdf`: the 2.x user manual for this release.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums of every file. Check them with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.

## Install

1. Back up your SD card.
2. Copy `PolyendTrackerOTv224.ptf` to `/Firmware` on the card. Keep the file name as it is: the updater only takes names of exactly 24 characters.
3. Go to **Config > Firmware update**, select the image and confirm. If the screen stays dark after the update, press the power button once.

**Going back:** keep the stock 1.9.2 image in `/Firmware` and install it the same way. From a running OpenTracker you can also write its file name into `/Firmware/ot_restore.txt` and power-cycle. Keep only the image you want, plus stock, in `/Firmware`: the rear reset button installs a `.ptf` from that folder.

## New in 2.2.4

- **Piano pad layout** (contributed by syamajala): Config > General > Pad layout: Stock / Piano. Piano turns the pads into two keyboards an octave apart, as on grid controllers: white keys along a row, black keys in the row above. The bottom-left C is the C of the project's root note octave, so change the root note to play higher or lower. The pad lights draw the keyboard: the scale's root brightest, white keys lit, black keys dimmer, keys outside the scale barely lit (still playable). Stock is the default and works as before.
- **Chords in step entry** (contributed by syamajala): in the pattern editor, in edit mode and stopped, select two or more tracks and press pads together: the notes fill the selected tracks left to right on the selection's first step, and the cursor stays. One Undo removes the whole chord. With a single-step cursor, pads enter one note at a time as before.
- **Synth pads play chords:** holding several pads on a synth instrument plays them all, up to the slot's Polyphony, on the pattern editor and the Synth page. Before, each new pad released the previous one. (Reported by idagani.)
- **Cleaner 44.1 kHz export** (contributed by bungoleena): the conversion to 44.1 kHz uses a 32-tap sinc filter. The slight wobble of the old conversion is gone and high frequencies come out much cleaner. Rendering takes longer: about +28 % for Export Song and +40 % for a selection; song stems barely change (+3 %). The 44117 Hz export mode is unchanged.
- **Sample Loader preview without gaps:** 24-bit and 32-bit float stereo files no longer drop out while you preview them.
- **Sample names with accents:** names such as "Più forte" show as "Piu forte" instead of stray symbols. Characters the font doesn't have show as `?`. The files keep their real names on the card.
- **Busy screens no longer fault** (contributed by syamajala): screens with many gradients or lots of text, such as the MIDI instrument CC page and the mixers, draw in full detail again.

Thanks to syamajala and bungoleena for their contributions, and to idagani for the report.

## New in 2.2.3

Colour themes, streaming long samples from the card, a steady MIDI clock on the DIN jack, pitch bend from a MIDI keyboard, synth stereo Width and many fixes. The full list is in the [2.2.3 release notes](https://github.com/opentracker-fw/opentracker-fw.github.io/releases/tag/v2.2.3).

## Still open

A couple of reported Performance screen issues, among them how the global Pattern length behaves in Song mode, need more work and testing. They come in a later release.

## The 2.x line

- **16 tracks:** tracks 13-16 work like MIDI tracks.
- **Three synth slots (S1-S3)** with five engines of our own sound design: ACD, VAP, PERC, FAT and WTFM. FX columns A-F drive six macros; Volume drives macro 7. A shared pool of 4 voices by default (1-8 in Config).
- **Projects in the Mini's format:** 16-track patterns, the three synth slots and `.patch` files. Older original-Tracker projects open as they are.
- **Stereo samples** in one-shot, loop, slice and beat-slice modes.
- **USB Storage mode:** the SD card shows up as a drive on a computer (about 1 MB/s).
- **Live resampling** (contributed by syamajala): record the mix, one track or Line In into a new instrument while the pattern plays.
- **Exports at 44.1 kHz**, a CPU meter, and a voice limiter that only ever cuts synth voices.

## Known limitations

- The synths follow the Mini's parameter sets, but the sound is our own design: close, not identical.
- The synth voice pool defaults to 4 because of the processor's speed.
- USB Storage mode: back up the card before first use. Linux systems without a desktop may need `partprobe` or a re-plug to see the card.
- Projects saved by 2.x use 16-track patterns. Stock firmware reads only tracks 1-8 of those patterns.
